ConfirmDialog

com.alecdorrington.laminarcomponents.ConfirmDialog
object ConfirmDialog

The page's one confirmation dialog, drawn by a single host at the root so that no ancestor of the asking control can clip it. Cancelling is the default.

Drawn as .lc-confirm-dialog over a .lc-confirm-backdrop, its question a .lc-confirm-subject over a .lc-confirm-consequence, and its buttons .lc-confirm-buttons in .lc-confirm-actions: .lc-confirm-cancel, and each answer .lc-confirm-answer, or .lc-confirm-danger if it destroys.
