Tooltip

com.alecdorrington.laminarcomponents.Tooltip
object Tooltip

The page's one tooltip, for every hint in place of title, which opens late and cannot be styled. A single host at the root draws it, so that no ancestor can clip it. It opens at once on hover, keyboard focus or a tap, beside the line of its element nearest the pointer, in the element's language.

Drawn as .lc-tooltip ([data-side='below'] when below its element), its content in a .lc-tooltip-body, a plain hint a .lc-tooltip-text.
