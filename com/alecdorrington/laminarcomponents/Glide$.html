Glide

com.alecdorrington.laminarcomponents.Glide
object Glide

Slides the elements a change moves from where they were drawn into their new places, rather than letting them jump there: where each stood is read before the change and again once it is laid out, and each that moved is drawn from where it stood and slid home. Elements are matched by keys of the caller's, so that one drawn afresh slides as the one it replaced. A block slides by transform; an inline element, which takes none, by its offsets, so it must be position: relative. Nothing slides for a reader who would rather nothing moved of itself.
